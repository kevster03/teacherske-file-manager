## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### Mistake #1: Too Many Ads
**Problem:** More ads ≠ more money. Too many = users leave.
**Solution:** Stick to 4-5 ad zones max.

### Mistake #2: Impatient Optimization
**Problem:** Changing settings every day confuses Ezoic AI.
**Solution:** Wait 30 days minimum before major changes.

### Mistake #3: Ignoring Mobile
**Problem:** 70% of traffic is mobile, but optimized for desktop only.
**Solution:** Test on phone, use responsive ad sizes.

### Mistake #4: Slow Page Speed
**Problem:** Ads + slow site = users bounce = no revenue.
**Solution:** Keep page load under 3 seconds (plugin already optimized ✅).

### Mistake #5: Not Tracking EPMV
**Problem:** Looking at RPM only (per page) instead of EPMV (per visitor).
**Solution:** Focus on increasing EPMV (session revenue).
